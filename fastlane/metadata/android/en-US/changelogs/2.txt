Works with far more schools' calendars.

- Your timetable now shows in your own time zone, not always Paris time
- Calendars that publish a weekly class as one repeating entry now show every week of it, including cancelled dates and moved sessions
- All-day entries (holidays, trips, exam periods) are no longer skipped
- Class times are read correctly whichever way your school writes them
- Session types are recognised in more languages, and the colour key now uses your school's own wording instead of the French TP/TD/CM
