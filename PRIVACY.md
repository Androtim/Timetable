# Privacy Policy — Timetable

_Last updated: 26 August 2026_

Timetable is an offline-first timetable viewer. **It collects no personal data whatsoever.**

- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.** The app contains no third-party
  analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.
- **Network access** is used for exactly one thing: downloading the iCal calendar file
  from the URL *you* configure. That request goes directly from your device to your
  school's server; no other server is ever contacted.
- **All data stays on your device.** The downloaded schedule, your group selection,
  your notes, and your widget preferences are stored only in the app's private local
  storage. They are never uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **No data sharing.** Since nothing is collected, nothing can be shared or sold.
- **About AI:** this app was *developed* with the assistance of AI (Anthropic's
  Claude), reviewed and tested by a human. The app itself contains **no AI
  features** and never sends any data to any AI service — or anywhere else.

If you have questions, open an issue at
<https://github.com/Androtim/Timetable/issues>.
